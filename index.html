About the Celebrant

Dr. Lemmy Omololu Omoyinmi has distinguished himself as one of Nigeria’s most renowned motivational and entrepreneurship speakers, widely recognized for his thought-provoking lectures and transformative training sessions. Over the years, he has conducted numerous workshops and seminars on broad managerial and social topics for some of the country’s leading corporate institutions—both locally and internationally.

A dynamic lecturer and consultant, Dr. Omoyinmi’s wealth of experience enables him to design lectures that challenge conventional thinking and inspire individuals and organizations to re-evaluate their approaches for better outcomes. He is a respected faculty member of the Lagos State University Business School and an International Consultant to the International Trade Centre (ITC)—the development partner for export success—and by extension, the World Trade Organization (WTO) and the United Nations (UN).

Beyond academia and consulting, Dr. Omoyinmi is the Director of NEKEGO Resources Limited, Chairman of the Cloisters Foundation and the Kemi Nelson Foundation, and an associate of Inside Integrity (South Africa), Micro-Enterprise Advisory Services (Sierra Leone), and Cross-Bridge Consult (Ghana). His extensive banking background with Barclays Bank (now Union Bank) has further enriched his expertise in finance, investment, and business development.

Dr. Omoyinmi is also a prolific author and scholar, with published works and seminar papers covering Finance, Security Market Analysis, Portfolio Management, Microfinance, and Microinsurance. As a regular facilitator for mandatory continuous professional development (MCPD) programs of leading professional institutes, he continues to shape minds and drive innovation across the corporate and academic landscapes.
